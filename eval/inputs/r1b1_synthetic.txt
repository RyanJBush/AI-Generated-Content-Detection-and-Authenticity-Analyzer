The committee met on a grey morning to review the quarterly maintenance plan. Each department submitted a short list of repairs, ordered by cost and urgency. Several members asked whether the older pumps could last another winter. The engineer replied that the bearings were worn but the housings were sound. After a long discussion, the group agreed to replace two pumps in the spring. A smaller team will inspect the roof drains and report back within a month.
